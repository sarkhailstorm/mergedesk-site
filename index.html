FAQ

Questions, answered

Does the customer get an email when a duplicate is closed?

Not with the default quiet close. MergeDesk briefly removes the duplicate's request type, which stops Jira Service Management sending the “resolved” email and satisfaction survey, then puts it back 5 minutes later. The customer still sees the request and a note on the portal. If you prefer the usual emails, switch to normal close in the settings.

What happens to replies sent to a merged request?

They're copied to the request you kept, as internal notes by default, so the customer isn't emailed their own words back.

Can I undo a merge?

Yes, for 7 days by default; your Jira admin can choose anything from 1 to 30. Undo deletes what the merge added, puts back any fields it changed and reopens the duplicates, without emailing the customer.

Will it merge requests just because the subjects match?

No. A matching subject alone only ever suggests a merge to an agent. Automatic merges need stronger evidence, such as the request's key in the subject or a reply in the same email thread. If the emails mention different order or ticket numbers, MergeDesk only suggests.

Who can merge requests?

Anyone who can edit and close both requests in Jira, checked before every merge and every undo. Admins can also limit merging to members of chosen groups.

Does it use AI?

No. Duplicates are found with clear rules you can see and change, so you always know why a request was flagged.

Which plans does it work with?

Every Jira Service Management Cloud plan, including Free. Sites with up to 10 users don't pay for MergeDesk, and everyone else gets a 30-day free trial.
