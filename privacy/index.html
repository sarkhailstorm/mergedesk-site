Privacy policy

MergeDesk runs inside your Atlassian site and keeps as little as it can. This page explains exactly what it stores, why, and for how long. Last updated 10 November 2026.

In short

Runs on Atlassian

No MergeDesk servers. Your data never leaves your Atlassian site.

We can't see your data

Request content is copied between your own requests and never stored by the app.

Account ids only

The only personal data is Atlassian account ids, removed when the undo window ends.

No tracking

No analytics, ads or cookies, in the app or on this website.

Who we are

MergeDesk for Jira Service Management is provided by Sarkhail Niyazi, trading as Findigo, India (“we”, “us”). You can reach us at support@findigo.in.

Your organization installs MergeDesk on its Atlassian site and decides how it is used. For data the app processes on that site, your organization is the controller.

Where MergeDesk runs

MergeDesk is built on Atlassian Forge and qualifies as “Runs on Atlassian”. Its code runs on Atlassian's infrastructure, its storage is Forge hosted storage on your site, and it has no permission to contact any outside server. That's why we have no way to see your requests, customers or settings.

What it doesn't do

  • It doesn't store request content: comments, descriptions and files are copied from one of your requests to another, inside Jira, and nowhere else.
  • It doesn't store names or email addresses.
  • It doesn't send data to us or to any third party.
  • It doesn't use AI services, analytics or advertising.

Personal data

The only personal data MergeDesk stores is Atlassian account ids, kept in merge records so a merge can be undone and in the history. As Atlassian requires, the app reports these ids to Atlassian every week and erases any that belong to closed accounts.

To access, correct or delete data the app holds about you, contact your Jira admin, who controls the site. You can also write to us and we'll help them.

Data residency: Forge hosted storage follows your site's data residency settings.

How long data is kept

  • Merge records: full details until the undo window ends; then account ids and step details are deleted and only request keys remain, for 180 days.
  • History: 90 days.
  • After uninstalling: Atlassian deletes the app's stored data under its Forge data retention policy. Content already merged into your requests stays, as it's part of your Jira data.

Sub-processors

None. Apart from Atlassian, which runs the Forge platform, no one processes data for MergeDesk.

This website

mergedesk.findigo.in uses no cookies, analytics or third-party scripts. If you pick light or dark mode, that choice is saved in your own browser and never sent anywhere. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you.

Changes

If we change what MergeDesk stores, we'll update this page and the date at the top before releasing the change.
